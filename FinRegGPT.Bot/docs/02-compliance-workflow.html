Compliance Automation Workflow
End-to-end flow: regulator publishes change → AI ingests → officer reviews → audit trail sealed. SLA per phase shown.
Phase 1 — Detection & Ingestion SLA: 24 hours
1Regulator publishes a rule change. Source: SEC, ESMA, FCA, MAS, PMDA, APRA, OSFI, or EDPB RSS feed. — Regulator
2Ingestion worker fetches and parses the change. Diff against previous version stored. — FinRegGPT.Bot ingestion worker
3AI classifier tags the change. Jurisdiction, regulator, category (AML, MiFID II, Basel III, GDPR, HIPAA, AI Act...), impact level, affected business units. — LLM (Z.ai)
4New Regulation row created with status = monitoring or pending. Audit log entry written. — Domain service
↓
Phase 2 — Impact Assessment SLA: 5 business days
5Compliance officer opens the regulation. AI presents a draft impact summary across affected business units. — Sarah Chen (CCO)
6AI suggests which policies need updating. Cross-references the regulation's category with the policy inventory and recent versions. — LLM (Z.ai)
7Risk items re-scored. Likelihood × impact recalculated; residual risk may shift. Worsening items trigger an alert. — Risk service
↓
Phase 3 — Policy Auto-Update & Review SLA: 15 business days
8AI generates a policy diff. Each affected policy receives an aiSuggestion field with proposed language, citation, and justification. — LLM (Z.ai)
9Owner reviews the suggestion. Accept, request changes, or dismiss. Accepted suggestions become a new draft version (e.g. v4.2.0 → v4.3.0). — Policy owner
10Draft routes to Risk Committee. Status = review → approved. All transitions logged with actor + timestamp. — Risk Committee
↓
Phase 4 — Publication & Training SLA: 5 business days post-approval
11Policy published. Status = published, version bumped, intranet updated. — Compliance ops
12Training refresh queued. Affected workforce segments enrolled in updated module; completion deadline set. — L&D system
↓
Phase 5 — Audit Trail Sealing SLA: Continuous (real-time)
13Every action appended to AuditLog with actor, action, target, severity, timestamp. Hash-chained to the previous entry. — Audit service
14Integrity check runs nightly. Hash mismatches (if any) trigger a critical alert to the CCO and Internal Audit. — Integrity checker
15Quarterly export signed by Internal Audit. CSV / PDF bundle archived for regulator inspection. — Internal Audit
Total cycle time: 25-30 business days from regulator publication to sealed audit trail. Manual touchpoints: steps 5, 9, 10, 11. Everything else is automated. AI is involved in 4 of 5 phases but never makes a publish decision — humans always review and sign off.
